LE GOLFE V23 — EDITORIAL DESK

1. Run supabase/editorial.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor (after community.sql).
2. Sign in with a moderator account.
3. Open /fr/editorial or /en/editorial.
4. Click “Actualiser les sources”.
5. Review/edit each candidate, then Publish or Reject.
6. Approved events appear in WHAT'S ON; approved news appears in LOCAL.

No new npm dependency.
